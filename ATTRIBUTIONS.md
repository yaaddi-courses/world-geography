# Image attributions

Country flags come from Wikimedia Commons (`File:Flag of ...svg`). Each file page was checked on 2026-10-02 through the Commons API: 192 are public domain; the flag of Oman is published under the Oman Open Government Licence (OGL-om 1.0), which allows reuse with attribution. Flags were downloaded through Special:FilePath, resized to fit a 256x256 canvas and framed with a thin grey border so white bands stay visible. The flag of Afghanistan is deliberately not used because the flag in use there is not recognised by every government; the course asks a geography fact instead.

Deck covers and the course cover were generated locally with FLUX.1-schnell from original prompts; no third-party artwork.
